Media moderation service — synthetic brief (fictional marketplace)

We run a second-hand marketplace where sellers upload photos and short videos of
the items they are listing. Listings appear immediately today, and our small
trust-and-safety team reviews complaints after the fact. That model does not
scale: we now receive a few thousand uploads on a normal day and roughly ten
times that during weekend promotions, when listing volume spikes for a few
hours and then drops back.

We want automated moderation of uploaded images and short videos before a
listing goes live. The system should flag content that clearly breaks our rules
— prohibited items, violent imagery, nudity, misleading photos — and route those
listings to a human for a final decision. Content that looks clean should be
published without waiting for a person.

Two things are still being debated internally. First, whether moderation must
finish before publication or whether listings can be published and then pulled
if a problem is found. Second, how long we are allowed to keep copies of the
uploaded media and the moderation decisions; our legal team has not settled this
yet.

We do not have a fixed traffic forecast beyond "spiky", and we have not measured
how long a single image or video analysis takes today.
