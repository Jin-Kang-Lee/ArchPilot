Customer support assistant — synthetic brief (fictional company)

We are a mid-sized online retailer with roughly 40 staff and we want to reduce
the load on our support inbox. Our agents currently answer around 10,000
customer conversations per day across chat and email, and most questions repeat
the same themes: delivery status, returns and exchanges, product sizing, and how
to reset a password.

We would like an AI assistant that answers customer questions using our internal
documentation — help-centre articles, policy pages and our returns process. The
content already exists but is spread across several systems and some of it is out
of date.

Our customers write in both English and Chinese, and we do not want to force
anyone into English. The assistant should reply in the language the customer
used.

These conversations contain sensitive customer data: names, order references,
partial addresses and, in some cases, details about a complaint. We are careful
about who can see that data.

Budget is limited for this project. We are a small team and we do not have
dedicated platform engineers, so whatever we build needs to be operated by the
same people who support customers today.
