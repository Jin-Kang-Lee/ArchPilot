Invoice processing — synthetic brief (fictional logistics firm)

Our finance team receives supplier invoices as PDF attachments by email. Today
one person opens each invoice, retypes the supplier name, invoice number, dates,
line items and totals into our accounting system, and then forwards anything
above a threshold to a manager for approval. In a busy month that is a few
thousand invoices, and the retyping is both slow and error-prone.

We would like the invoice to be read automatically. The system should take the
PDF, pull out the fields we care about, and store the result so our finance team
can look it up later. If the extraction looks uncertain — for example the totals
do not add up, or a field is missing — the invoice should not be posted silently;
someone from finance needs to check it. Once an invoice is accepted, the
supplier should be told that we have received it, and invoices over our approval
threshold need to reach the right manager.

Invoices arrive throughout the month with a lump at month end. We have not
decided whether the original PDFs must be kept for audit, and we have not
settled how long extracted data should stay in the system. We also do not know
yet whether any of our suppliers send scanned paper invoices rather than digital
PDFs, which may change what the reading step has to cope with.
